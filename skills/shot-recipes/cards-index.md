# Shot Recipes — English Index

50 curated recipe cards from video-shotcraft's 157. The cards themselves are in
Chinese (the original author's wording); timings/parameters are frame-exact and
language-neutral. This index gives you the English one-liner + typical duration
at 30fps + energy so you can **pick a named shot** without reading every card.

Rules of selection (from the source project):
- One technique stars once per film (a technique may be the "lead" of only one shot).
- Hold/breathe frames are budgeted FIRST, then the move is placed.
- Cards marked "技法卡" (technique card) are pick-lists, not shots — read one to
  choose a transition style for a seam, it doesn't occupy an energy slot.
