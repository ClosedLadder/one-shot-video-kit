## Why this cut
Explainers are a core one-shot job (scoreboard brief 3 is a Vox-style explainer).
The script-to-screen workflow plus diagram/whiteboard/isometric recipes cover the
genre; scene-grammar gives a reusable vocabulary for sequencing explainers.
