# Style Backlog — 20–30 candidate styles, priority order

**Count:** 24 entries — 3 baked (marked `BAKED`), 21 to build.

## Build-order rationale

Styles are ranked by three criteria, in this order:

1. **Brief-type coverage.** The scoreboard briefs are logo sting, character loop,
   vox explainer, product ad, short film. A style that unlocks a brief type the
   baked three can't do (on-screen typography, photoreal product, UGC) outranks a
   style that overlaps them.
2. **Distinctiveness from the baked three.** Watercolor-cartoon owns handmade
   warmth, vox-explainer owns bold documentary cut-outs, cinematic-mixed owns
   polished commercial realism. Near-neighbours (e.g. "ink sketch with less wash")
   rank below genuinely new registers.
3. **Feasibility across all three pipelines.** Code-film first (the kit's $0 home
   turf), then mixed-flow, then ai-footage. A style must be producible in at least
   two pipelines to earn a slot; the top of the list is feasible in all three.

Tiers in the list: **Tier 1** fills obvious brief-type gaps cheaply in code-film;
**Tier 2** is mixed-flow/ai-footage friendly with strong brief coverage;
**Tier 3** is useful but narrower, harder, or slop-prone (build with extra review).
