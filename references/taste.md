# Taste — anti-slop laws for the kit

These laws override model instincts. They are distilled from the kit's scoreboard
rubric, proof-frame checklist, and style briefs. For enterprise/web launch styles,
also read `skills/brand-launch/rules/taste.md`.

## I — Composition & hierarchy

1. **One focal point per frame.** Squint test: is #1 obvious, #2 second?
2. **No two adjacent beats share a visual formula.** Each beat gets its own world.
3. **One world, one hand.** Palette, medium, and linework stay consistent unless a deliberate arc is scripted.
4. **Every shot is an event.** First frame vs last frame: something changed.

## II — Motion & timing

5. **Nothing ever still.** Idles idle, cameras drift, linework boils.
6. **Faces act, never snap.** Emotion changes use anticipation/take/overshoot.
7. **Reads land in order.** One read at a time; cause before reaction.
8. **Beats on beats.** Cuts land on downbeats; no continuous visual pulse synced to BGM.
9. **Every seam has a transition.** No hard start, no dead stop; vary the vocabulary.

## III — Character & cartoon

10. **Model sheets are law** when a brief names a character pack under `characters/`.
11. **Karaoke band only** for on-screen text in cartoon styles: lower third, never over faces/action.
12. **Text reading time:** visible frames ≥ words × 10 frames/word (@30fps) × 1.5 breathing room.

## IV — Slop blacklist (instant tells)

13. Purple/violet gradient backgrounds or blue-to-purple schemes.
14. 3-column icon-in-circle feature grids.
15. Center-aligned everything; uniform border-radius on every element.
16. Decorative blobs, wavy dividers, emoji as design elements.
17. Gradient text on metrics; "Unlock the power of..." hero copy.
18. Expanding ring / ripple / burst-circle motion for impact.
19. Heartbeat / rhythmic scale oscillation on the whole frame.
20. Text overlaid on full-bleed motion assets (unless brand evidence says otherwise).

## V — Ship gate

21. **Slop test:** Would a stranger believe "AI made this" immediately? If yes, redesign.
22. **Remove test:** Cover each element mentally — does the frame improve? Delete what doesn't earn its pixels.
23. **Proof frames from the final file** — not previews, not intermediates (`skills/self-review/proof-checklist.md`).
